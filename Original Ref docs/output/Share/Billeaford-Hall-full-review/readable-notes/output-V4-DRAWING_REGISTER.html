Audit conclusions

  • Existing P02 plan, four elevations and section are present; their annotations explicitly identify uncertainties. Reissuing them as verified demolition drawings now would hide missing evidence.
  • The P02 proposed layout is compatible with the selected one-enclosed/two-open direction. V3-A's two-closed layout and V3-B's charcoal roof are not the current choice.
  • P02's 300 mm plinth is a visual assumption. S17 prefers three courses but allows reconsideration to keep the building low; actual brick/mortar dimensions and coordinated levels are still pending.
  • At 6000 mm span and 2300 mm eaves, 30 degrees gives about 4032 mm ridge. P02 retains 4163 mm and derives about 31.84 degrees. Choose controlling measured/supplier geometry before issuing coordinated plans.
  • S17 confirms the new entrance faces east. Existing north/south reference labels remain unverified; plot orientation must be related to the measured map before issuing compass-labelled sheets.
  • S17 confirms red clay pantiles and natural timber/doors; no specific vehicle checks are required. Equal bays replace the Model Y clearance question. General-builder construction is the intended route; no kit selection is needed.
  • Height limits are researched in HEIGHT_AND_CONSTRUCTION.md. The existing 4163 mm ridge is not an adopted V4 height. Site eligibility, nearest boundary distance, ground/floor datum and tile-compatible roof geometry must be settled before a coordinated issue.
  • Four new site-independent outputs are useful now: mapping advice, the input workbook, this audit/register, and the image trial. A measured map cannot be reconstructed from the available screenshot.
