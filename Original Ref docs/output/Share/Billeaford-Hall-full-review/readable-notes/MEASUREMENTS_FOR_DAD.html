Height research - answered; final design still pending

For an eligible dual-pitched outbuilding, the national Class E limits are 4 m overall and 2.5 m eaves. If any part is within 2 m of the house's curtilage boundary, the overall limit is 2.5 m. There is no 4.2 m allowance. Height on a slope uses the highest ground immediately adjacent to the building. The relevant curtilage is not necessarily the whole estate. Government technical guidance.
