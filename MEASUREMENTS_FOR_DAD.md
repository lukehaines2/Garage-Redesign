# Measurements and decisions for Dad

Use this with the P01 review pack. The drawings currently reproduce reference dimensions, not a measured survey.

## First: identify the site

- [ ] Confirm address/postcode and which house the garage belongs to. The proposed reference address and map labels appear different.
- [ ] Mark the existing garage, proposed rectangle, vehicle entrance and access route on the map.
- [ ] Identify the other buildings and confirm north, boundaries and ownership/access arrangements.
- [ ] Supply the original uncropped drawings if available, and photos of all four sides.

## Existing garage

- [ ] Wall footprint: confirm 6800 long x 3500 wide. Note where each tape measurement starts/ends.
- [ ] Roof length: confirm 7200 and measure each overhang separately.
- [ ] Height at eaves and ridge: confirm 2200 / 3800 against one stated ground datum. Use safe ground-based measurement or a surveyor.
- [ ] Door opening: measure full clear width and height. Does the source's 1100 refer to one leaf?
- [ ] Confirm positions of all doors/windows, including the cropped side elevation.
- [ ] Concrete approach: confirm 1200 depth and actual width/shape.
- [ ] Note sloping ground and existing finishes.

## Proposed cart lodge

- [ ] Confirm 9000 x 6000 and three nominal 3000 divisions.
- [ ] Confirm left-hand paired doors when facing the building, and a full internal partition.
- [ ] Choose what controls the roof: the 4163 ridge or 30-degree pitch. At a 6000 span and 2300 eaves, 30 degrees gives about 4032 ridge.
- [ ] Confirm what “eaves height” refers to: roof surface, beam top, or underside. P01 uses roof surface at the wall line.
- [ ] Confirm roof overhangs, material, plinth height and door appearance.
- [ ] Give desired clear door/open-bay sizes and the real car's dimensions, including mirrors and opening height.
- [ ] Measure proposed corners to fixed buildings/boundaries, and any rotation or movement from the old garage.
- [ ] Confirm surface levels, drainage and rainwater disposal intentions.

Record: measurement | value in mm | start/end points or datum | date | measured by | photo/sketch reference.

The 150 mm posts, 180 mm beam depth, 100 mm walls, 300 mm plinth and 150 mm proposed overhangs are visual assumptions. They are not structural specifications.
